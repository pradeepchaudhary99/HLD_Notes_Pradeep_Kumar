Week 5-6 — Messaging, Streaming & Payments

The sessions on real-time messaging and money movement. Kafka gets a from-first-principles deep dive as the streaming backbone; the WhatsApp message flow is broken down as an interactive animation. The polished, interview-ready write-ups built on this material live in WhatsApp — Messaging at Scale and Payment Service under Practice Questions.

  • Kafka, In Depth — Theory, Failure Modes & ExercisesKafka as a distributed, append-only commit log — partitioning, replication & ISR, consumer groups and rebalancing, at-most / at-least / exactly-once semantics, Docker + KRaft setup, a full CLI reference, and hands-on exercises that make you break the mental model on purpose→
  • WhatsApp Message Flow — Interactive WalkthroughClick or play through the real send → persist → publish → deliver path with live Redis connection-registry state and pub/sub events shown as they happen — covering online delivery across gateways, the persist-only offline path, and group fan-out over shared gateways→
How this fits together
The Kafka note is the transport-layer background; the WhatsApp animation shows one concrete application of a hash-partitioned pub/sub bus plus a connection registry. Read the deep dives here first, then work the two Practice Questions cold before comparing.
